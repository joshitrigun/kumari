# The King's Regret — Production Prompts (AI Video)

## 🎯 Golden Rule for Dreamina Video (First-Frame `16:9` Workflow)

All 17 approved character and keyframe plates have been formatted to native **`2560×1440` (`16:9`)** widescreen in `production/character_sheets/approved/16x9/`, preserving 100% of each vertical painting with dark candlelit temple-shadow side wings.

When generating video clips in **Dreamina (Image-to-Video / First Frame)**:
1. **Switch Mode from `Omni reference` to `First and Last Frame`:** In the bottom Dreamina Video bar (next to `Dreamina Seedance 2.0 Mini`), make sure the mode dropdown is set to **`First and Last Frame`** — **NOT `Omni reference`**. (`Omni reference` treats your PNG only as a loose character inspiration and redraws the whole scene from scratch, which de-ages the King by 15–20 years and changes his crown and room!)
2. **Upload First Frame:** Select the shot's widescreen plate from `production/character_sheets/approved/16x9/*.png` in the **First Frame** slot.
3. **Leave Last Frame Empty:** Never combine two different compositions as First + Last Frame (that causes mid-shot object pop-in and face morphing).
4. **Set Aspect Ratio to `16:9`** and Duration to **`5s` or `10s`** as noted per shot.
5. **Use Pure-Motion Prompts Only for Video:** Because the `paubha` painting style, brushwork, and character identity are already 100% baked into your `16:9` First Frame, **do NOT include `"Newar paubha painting"` or the `[STYLE BLOCK]` in Dreamina Video prompts**. Describing painting styles in the video prompt triggers Dreamina Video to hallucinate new wall paintings (including Buddhist monk medallions) as the camera moves. Use the `[STYLE BLOCK]` **only** when generating new still images.
6. **The Proven `shot02a` "Living Portrait" 4-Layer Formula:** To get the exact same living-painting magic and 100% facial identity lock as `shot02a_goddess_appears_solo.mp4`, every character video prompt uses four locked layers:
   - **Layer 1 (5% Micro Camera Push-In):** `"Very slow, subtle camera push-in of only 5 percent toward..."` *(gives cinematic parallax without zooming far enough to trigger AI face re-synthesis)*.
   - **Layer 2 (100% Identity & Background Lock):** `"Keep the camera distance almost locked so [her/his] face,matte painted skin texture, and bold ink linework remain 100 percent identical to the first frame with zero morphing or redrawing. Keep the dark side margins and background completely plain, dark, and unchanged with no smoke and no fog."`
   - **Layer 3 (Sacred vs. Mortal Lighting Rule):** **Only Goddess Taleju (and the Kumari) possesses a divine golden aura (*prabha-mandala*).** The mortal King is lit solely by warm earthly *sukunda* oil-lamp candlelight (`"Warm amber candlelight from the small brass oil lamps flickers softly across the silk folds of his robes while his forehead, crown, and skin remain matte hand-painted mineral pigment with zero glowing aura, zero forehead shine, and no specular light beams."`)
   - **Layer 4 (Single Micro-Expression):** One gentle blink (`"eyelids close and open once in a slow, serene blessing"`) or a slow breath — never large head turns or complex finger choreography.
7. **Wide Two-Shot Face Protection (`Shot 2B`, `Shot 3`, `Shot 6C`):** Whenever a figure's face is small in a wide two-shot (`<80px` tall), Dreamina's video decoder lacks enough pixels to preserve `paubha` features if the head moves. Keep wide two-shots focused on lighting/lamp/hand motion, and if Dreamina softens a background face, we apply our **Tracked First-Frame Head Composite** in post so her face stays 100% identical to `taleju_act1_front.png`.
