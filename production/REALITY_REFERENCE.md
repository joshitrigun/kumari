# The King's Regret - Reality Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-06-14

These notes translate public historical and cultural references into production direction. They are not a substitute for a Newar cultural consultant, but they should keep the film closer to the known tradition and away from generic palace fantasy.

## Jayaprakash Malla

Useful historical read:

- Last Malla king of Kantipur/Kathmandu before the Shah conquest.
- Politically embattled: rivalry with brothers, nobles, exile, restoration, and final defeat.
- Not only a ruler: associated with Newar-language literary work and court culture.
- Personality should combine refinement and danger: cultured, devotional, proud, suspicious, volatile under threat.
- The story should not make him a simple romantic hero. He is a king whose desire and entitlement damage his relationship with the sacred.

Visual direction:

- Malla/Newar courtly, not Malaysian or generic Southeast Asian palace.
- Tall, strong-built, handsome 50-year-old Newar Malla king in his prime with a firm square jaw, high cheekbones, keen deep-set dark eyes, and a well-groomed short beard.
- Use a tight-fitting deep madder-dyed cotton upper garment (tapalan) with gold-thread borders, thick wound waist cloth (patuka), pleated lower skirt-wrap (jama), hammered gold Tayo necklace, and a multi-tiered gold repoussé headpiece sitting low on dark brushed-back hair (no turban).
- His dignity should be controlled and magnetic in Act 1.
- In Act 2, make the crack small and interior: one loose hair strand across his temple, warm devotion overflowing into human longing in his eyes (not a melodramatic villain face), followed by the room's warmth collapsing into cold slate-blue as he reaches.
- In Act 3, remove crown and gold regalia, but keep his tall, dignified stature (never a beggar or emaciated). He wears simple undyed cream cotton robes on his palace terrace at misty dawn, praying across the square toward Taleju's temple.
- In Act 4, dress him in a simple Nepali cotton shawl over plain robes — humbled and at peace as he lowers himself among the people before the newborn Kumari.

Personality keywords: refined, devout, proud, intelligent, suspicious, wounded, volatile, finally humbled.

## Queen Daya Lakshmi / The Queen Figure

Useful historical read:

- Jayaprakash Malla's queen consort is recorded as Daya Lakshmi / Dayavati.
- Their relationship in historical accounts is politically fraught; she is not just decorative palace background.
- In this film, she works best as witness: the human eye that sees sacred order becoming fragile.

Visual direction:

- Newar **queen** in royal court dress — not a generic sari queen, and **not** the common Haku Patasi (that black-cotton-with-red-border sari is everyday Newar/Jyapu dress, not royal).
- Dress her in refined court fabrics: a deep wine/plum **silk sari** with woven **gold-thread (kastha) borders** and **brocade (kinkhab)**, an embroidered **silk cholo**, a gold-brocade patuka, and a maroon **makhamali (velvet)** veil-shawl. Plum/gold keeps her distinct from Taleju's vermillion temple brocade.
- Jewelry should signal Newar royalty, using authentic Newar pieces (avoid the pan-Nepali/hill **Bulaki** septum nose-ring): the grand **Tayo** (serpent-headed lozenge-pendant ceremonial necklace), a **Naugedi** gold-bead necklace and **Kantha** choker, a **Luswa/Lunswan** gold head-disk with **Nyapu Shikha** (five gold chains across the hair), a **Sirbandi** head-binding, a **Tikma** forehead ornament, heavy basket-drop **Makasi** earrings, and gold bangles — repoussé gold with coral/turquoise, catching candlelight.
- Her face should be partially hidden by ankhijhyal lattice. The lattice is her mask and her prison.

Personality keywords: perceptive, restrained, aching, politically aware, spiritually sensitive, not jealous, not accusatory.

## Taleju Bhawani

Useful cultural read:

- Royal tutelary goddess of the Malla kings.
- Tantric manifestation of Durga / Tripura Sundari; linked to royal legitimacy and protection.
- The dice/game legend is about divine counsel, taboo, and withdrawal of legitimacy, not romance.
- She appears **in human form** — a living, moving woman he can sit across from and play dice with — but her **aura is a goddess's**: sacred authority wearing a human form, never a mortal woman.

Visual direction:

- A "goddess in a perfect human form": a serene woman of flawless, idealized human beauty — perfect human face and gracefully proportioned human body, **warm human skin** (not gilded or glowing). Her divinity shows through an aura *around* her — a faint gold nimbus, a soft gold rim-light, the Shri Yantra, and a sovereign, uncanny stillness — not a rigid temple statue, and not merely mortal. Flawless beauty, but never sexualized.
- Keep her compassionate, but never casual or lover-like.
- Add copper Shri Yantra / triangular mandala geometry behind her head and a faint gold-leaf nimbus as subtle sacred design language.
- Deep vermillion red and hammered antique gold Newar ceremonial brocade (Kinkhab), three-tiered gold repoussé temple crown studded with coral and turquoise, luminous red bindi, refined kohl — never glamour makeup.
- When she recoils, she is wounded boundary and withdrawn legitimacy, not anger for anger's sake.

Personality keywords: compassionate, sovereign, watchful, protective, boundary-setting, luminous, beyond possession.

## Kumari

Useful cultural read:

- Living embodiment of Taleju, traditionally a young prepubescent Newar girl from Shakya/Bajracharya communities.
- Royal Kumari is associated with red and gold garments, topknot, agni-chakshu/fire-eye forehead makeup, stillness, and rare public appearances.
- Stillness and composure matter: her silence is powerful.
- Her feet traditionally do not touch the ground outside the residence; in this film she is a newborn infant who never walks or grips objects — she is carried ceremonially across a white cloth path or rests in complete stillness on an embroidered cushion with the lotus token placed on her chest.

Visual direction:

- Red/gold Newar Kumari ceremonial attire.
- Hand-painted kohl and agni-chakshu/fire-eye makeup should look organic, not superhero mask or theatrical face paint.
- She should be physically small against architecture. The burden is large; the child is small.
- Her glow should be dawn-like and restrained, not Taleju's full divine aura.

Personality keywords: silent, composed, grave, innocent, protected, sacred, not cute, not spooky.

## Production Guardrails

- Avoid Malaysian, Thai, Balinese, generic princess, or generic royal fantasy silhouettes.
- Avoid romantic framing between King and Taleju. Use devotional/sacred framing.
- Avoid sexualized Taleju or Kumari imagery.
- Avoid making the Queen jealous. She is a witness to sacred rupture.
- Avoid making Kumari theatrical or frightening. Her power is stillness.
- Use Kathmandu Valley architecture, Newar lattice, brick, wood carving, pagoda roofs, and Taleju temple visual language as the shared world.
